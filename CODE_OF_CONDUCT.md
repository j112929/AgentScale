# Community conduct

Participate respectfully and focus criticism on ideas and code. Harassment, threats,
discrimination, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing and publication of private data are
not acceptable. Apply these expectations in issues, reviews and project discussions.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and limit participation. For conduct
concerns, use a private contact already provided by a maintainer; if none is available,
request a private channel without publishing sensitive details. Maintainers should
handle reports discreetly and fairly and avoid conflicts of interest.
